# Mybizz CS — Feature List

Every feature a client of Mybizz CS receives, by category. This list is the single source for what Mybizz CS offers, for the build and for marketing.

## 1. Price and terms

- £79 a month, one flat price, charged in the client's own currency: £79, $99, C$135, €89 or CHF 89.
- Unlimited staff at no extra cost.
- Month-to-month, with no contract; cancel at any time, effective at the end of the paid month.
- No add-ons: AI, voice, email, accounts and hosting are all included.
- The client pays only the payment gateway's own fee on each payment. Mybizz adds no markup.
- The client can leave at any time with all its data.

## 2. Trying Mybizz before buying

- Demo app (`demo.mybizz.live`): a full working business loaded with sample data.
- Try app (`try.mybizz.live`): an empty Mybizz for exploring setup.
- Both apps reset every night, so visitors can change or break anything.
- Demo emails are really sent, and every one goes to the visitor's own email address, so visitors see exactly what their customers would receive.
- Payments run in the gateways' test mode (Stripe and PayPal), so visitors pay with a test card and watch the whole flow without any real money moving.

## 3. Getting started

- Self-registration creates the client's reference number.
- An onboarding wizard takes the client through setup one step at a time, with a progress indicator and a help video on every step. The steps, in order:
  1. App language
  2. Business profile
  3. Brand palette
  4. System currency
  5. Legal acknowledgement
  6. Payment gateway
  7. Connect the client's domain, for the website and for email
  8. Import existing contacts from HubSpot, Mailchimp, Acuity or any CSV file, with ready-made column matching
- Setup can be left and resumed without losing progress.
- The client's Cloudflare R2 storage is connected automatically during registration.
- AI writes the first website text from the business profile.
- Every step of setup has a short help video, opened from its tooltip in a pop-up, so the client sets everything up without help.
- Assisted setup, where Mybizz configures everything, costs one month's subscription, once.

## 4. Team, roles and access

- Unlimited staff logins.
- Five roles: Owner, Manager, Admin, Staff and Customer. Each role sees only what it is allowed to see.
- Every permission is enforced on the server, so a user can't get around it by tampering with the browser.
- Sign-in with email and password, password reset, rate-limited sign-in and account lockout.

## 5. Website and public presence

- Pages: Home, About, Contact, Services listing and detail, Blog, Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Cookie Policy.
- Contact form feeds straight into the CRM, with an optional Google Maps location.
- Homepage templates: Classic, Services, Booking, Minimalist and more.
- Parallax hero, carousels, and video from YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion or a direct link.
- Mobile-friendly, search-engine optimised, with clean web addresses.
- Runs on the client's own domain.
- Owners and Managers edit website pages on the page itself: an Edit button makes every text, image and video editable where it sits, AI writes or rewrites any text on request, and Save publishes the changes immediately.

## 6. Landing pages

- Three templates: Lead Capture, Event Registration, Video Sales Letter.
- Views and conversions tracked per page.
- New landing pages are set up in the landing page editor, where the template and tracking are chosen.
- Owners and Managers edit a landing page on the page itself: an Edit button makes every text, image and video editable where it sits, AI writes or rewrites any text on request, and Save publishes the changes immediately.

## 7. Blog

- Rich text editor with three post layouts, pullquotes, featured image and video.
- SEO settings per post, draft/scheduled/published/archived statuses, categories and view counts.
- New posts are written in the blog editor, where layout, SEO and scheduling are set.
- Owners and Managers fix published posts on the page itself with the Edit button.
- Comment moderation, switchable on or off.
- A Share button on every published post sends the post to Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp or Instagram in one tap, with an AI-written caption in the client's language and a full preview of the post.
- Readers share posts too, with share buttons for Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Copy link.
- Visits and new contacts from every shared post are counted per platform.

## 8. Services

- Time-based pricing (a 60-minute session) and deliverable pricing (a logo design).
- Each service carries: category, duration, delivery mode, provider, images, video, meeting types, intake form and active status.

## 9. Bookings and scheduling

- One booking flow: service → provider → date and time → meeting type → intake form → payment → confirmation.
- Calendar in month, week and day views, colour-coded by status.
- Business hours, provider schedules, blocked dates and exceptions.
- Meeting types: at the business, at the customer's location (with travel time), video call (link sent automatically), phone call.
- Group sessions, and rooms or equipment booked with the appointment.
- Payment per service: in full, deposit, on the day, or by invoice with payment terms.
- Reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before.
- Recurring appointments, rescheduling and special requests.
- Bookings are cancelled, never deleted.
- Each provider has their own calendar, services, availability and revenue figures.

## 10. Packages, orders and time

- Prepaid session packages, with the remaining balance visible to both the client and the customer.
- Fixed-price orders, paid online, tracked as ordered, in progress or delivered.
- Time tracking per provider, customer and service. Billable time turns into invoice lines.

## 11. Sales

- Deals pipeline: sales stages from lead to won or lost, with values and owners.
- Companies: a business customer with several contacts.
- Quotes: a priced quote that the customer accepts online, which becomes an invoice.

## 12. Invoicing and payments

- Automatic invoices from bookings, orders, packages, time and accepted quotes.
- Manual invoices for any other work.
- Payment terms, overdue reminders and sequential numbering.
- Lifecycle: draft, sent, paid, overdue, cancelled.
- Tax rate, label and registration number set by the client.
- PDF invoices, sent by email.
- Payment recording and refunds.
- Gateways: Stripe for cards and PayPal, each with test and live modes.
- A system currency for accounting, plus an optional display currency per service.

## 13. CRM and contacts

- One record per person (Lead, Customer or Inactive), with a full activity timeline, including every email.
- Lifetime value, total bookings, average spend and last contact date.
- Contacts created automatically from bookings, enquiries, forms and emails.
- Ready-made segments, plus a custom segment builder.
- CSV import and export.

## 14. Email

- Unlimited email addresses on the client's own domain: personal (`john@abc.com`) and desk (`help@`, `marketing@`, `accounts@`).
- A Team Inbox inside Mybizz: each staff member sees their own address and their team's desks.
- Each person's inbox works like a full mail program: Inbox, Drafts, Sent, Archive, Spam and Trash; reply, reply all and forward; sort, filter and search; star and mark as unread; and act on many emails at once.
- Conversations grouped, and an AI-drafted reply on request.
- Every email from a known contact appears on that contact's timeline. An unknown sender becomes a lead in one click.
- Spam and virus filtering on every incoming email.
- The Team Inbox works in the phone browser, from the same home-screen shortcut as voice.
- Confirmations, reminders, invoices and receipts sent from the client's own domain.
- Newsletters, campaigns and ready-made sequences, sent to segments, with open and click tracking.
- One-click unsubscribe, with bounces and complaints blocked automatically.
- Every email signed (DKIM) so it reaches the inbox, not spam. Mybizz sets up the domain automatically.
- Old emails kept in the client's own storage, searchable, and part of the client's data on exit.
- No email account to set up and no email bill.

## 15. Lead capture and funnels

- Lead capture forms that create the lead and the contact together.
- Leads tracked from source to paying customer.
- New leads enrolled automatically in sequences.
- Blog post → landing page → booking as one connected funnel.

## 16. Tasks and automation

- Manual tasks linked to contacts.
- Automatic tasks, such as "follow up an enquiry that did not book" or "ask for a review after an appointment".
- Automation rules: when an event happens and conditions are met, Mybizz creates a task, sends an email or enrols a contact in a sequence.

## 17. Reviews and legal pages

- An email asks each customer for a star rating and a comment after the appointment.
- The client approves each review before it shows on the website.
- The client can type in testimonials the business already has.
- Clause Builder: the Privacy Policy, Terms and Cookie Policy are built from pre-written clauses and published instantly, and every published version is kept.
- Customers accept the Terms and the Privacy Policy with a tick box whenever they book, order, buy, pay, sign up or accept a quote.
- A cookie banner asks every visitor before any map or video sets its cookies, and every choice is recorded.

## 18. Customer portal

- Customers sign in to see My Dashboard, My Bookings, My Packages, My Invoices, My Reviews and Account.
- Customers book, reschedule, pay, accept quotes and update their own details.

## 19. Dashboard and reports

- Revenue, bookings and customer totals, compared period on period.
- Trend charts and an activity feed.
- Revenue by service and by provider, and marketing return on spend.
- PDF and CSV export.
- Reports scheduled by email.

## 20. AI assistant

- One AI helper on every relevant page, in a floating window.
- Ten assists:
  - bio and profile writer
  - service descriptions
  - FAQs
  - proposals
  - blog drafts
  - email replies
  - invoice messages
  - terms explained in plain language
  - client sentiment summary
  - booking and availability insights
- AI also writes website text in edit mode and social media captions for every published post.
- Every result can be copied, edited and inserted into its form.
- Client sentiment and booking insights work from the first day on sample data, and on the client's own data once the sample data is cleared.
- The application never depends on the AI.

## 21. Voice (WhatsUp)

- Works from any phone browser, saved as a home-screen shortcut. No app to install.
- Speak to any AI assist instead of typing.
- Create tasks by voice.
- Ask questions and hear the answers: bookings, payments, tasks, customers, and navigation ("Show me unpaid invoices").
- Spoken morning briefing of 20 to 30 seconds, switchable on or off.
- Follow-up questions within one conversation.
- A natural-sounding voice.
- Voice form filling: say "New booking — Sarah Johnson, Swedish massage, Friday 2pm" and the form fills itself. Nothing is saved until the user confirms. This works for bookings, contacts, invoices, tasks, quotes and deals.
- Business intelligence by voice: ask about best services, top clients, clients who haven't booked in 60 days, retention, the best campaign, the busiest days or popular time slots, or whether the business is on track. Answers are worked out from the client's own figures, and no customer name is ever sent to the AI.

## 22. Sample data

- A new client starts with three months of sample data for a business in the client's own trade, so every report, AI assist and voice question shows results from day one.
- A banner on every screen shows when sample data is present.
- The Owner clears all the sample data with one button, and everything the client created stays.
- Sample data never sends an email, takes a payment or appears in the client's accounts export.

## 23. Accounts

- Built-in accounts: invoices, payments, refunds and expenses in one place, with a photo of every receipt.
- Reports: profit and loss by month, outstanding invoices, tax collected and tax paid, and customer statements.
- One-click export to Wave, Xero or QuickBooks: one button for each, downloading the period's full books, ready to import.
- No accounting subscription needed.

## 24. Data ownership and backups

- Backups, history, emails and attachments are kept in the client's own Cloudflare R2 storage, each in its own folder: daily and weekly backups, history, the activity log, payment messages, emails, receipts and exports.
- Backups run daily: the last 7 daily and 4 weekly backups are kept.
- History and activity log entries older than 30 days, and emails older than 90 days, move to R2 and stay viewable.
- The client views history in Settings, and Mybizz restores from R2 on request.
- All data exports as CSV at any time.
- On leaving, the client gets a full export and the decrypted Vault in its own R2 storage.

## 25. Security and compliance

- The Vault: an encrypted store for every credential. Each view needs an authenticator-app code, and the owner is emailed on every access.
- Full audit trail of every significant action.
- Each client is in its own app with its own database, completely separate from every other client.
- Aligned with the UK GDPR, the client's own privacy law and PCI DSS.

## 26. Design and ease of use

- Material Design 3, refined for a polished look.
- Eight brand palettes, changeable at any time.
- Dark mode on every screen.
- Works on every screen size.
- Every feature has a short help video, opened from its tooltip in a pop-up, shown in the language the client chose for the app.
- The client chooses the app's language: English, French, German, Spanish or Italian. Screens, emails and help videos all follow that choice. English is available at launch, and the other languages follow one at a time.

## 27. Hosting and updates

- Each client runs in its own app with its own database, hosted by Mybizz.
- Improvements reach every client automatically, with nothing for the client to install.
- Mybizz monitors each client's health around the clock.

## 28. Support

- A Help button in every app sends a ticket or feedback straight to Mybizz.
- Response within 24 hours on business days.
- A problem that stops the business is acknowledged within 4 business hours.
- Mybizz is alerted when a client goes quiet (14 days with no staff sign-in, or 30 days with no bookings), so the founder can step in.
