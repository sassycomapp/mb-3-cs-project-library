## 2. What Mybizz CS Is

**Source:** `mb-3-cs-concept.md` §1, §5, §7; `mb-3-cs-feature-list.md`

Mybizz CS is not a booking app, a CRM, a website builder or an invoicing tool. It is the whole operating system of a consulting or service business.

Each client receives its own isolated application instance, on its own domain, with its own database. The instance includes:

- A professional public website, with homepage templates, page templates, a blog and landing pages
- Booking and scheduling for every way a service is delivered
- Payment processing through the client's own Stripe and PayPal accounts
- CRM and contact management
- A sales pipeline, companies and quotes
- Email on the client's own domain: personal addresses, shared desks, the Team Inbox and email marketing
- Invoicing and accounts
- Time tracking
- Analytics and a business dashboard
- Reviews and testimonials
- A customer portal
- AI assistance for writing and insight
- Voice access to the business
- Daily backups and full data export into the client's own storage

Mybizz CS replaces several separate subscriptions, the integrations between them and the technical setup they demand.

## 3. The Problem Mybizz CS Solves

**Source:** `mb-3-cs-concept.md` §2

A small consulting or service business that wants to operate professionally online buys and wires together five to seven separate tools: a website and hosting, a booking system, a payment gateway, a CRM, an email marketing platform and an invoicing tool. Each has its own subscription, learning curve and support.

The problem has five compounding parts:

1. **Money.** Several subscriptions and setup costs, with more added for every member of staff.
2. **Integration.** The tools do not share customer, booking, payment or invoice data, so the owner re-types data between them.
3. **Technical burden.** The owner learns and runs several systems and becomes a part-time IT administrator.
4. **Data.** Client records are spread over three or four systems, and there is no reliable view of the business.
5. **Ownership.** On marketplace platforms, the business's customers are effectively the platform's customers, visibility is controlled by the platform, and commissions reach 20–23.5% on new customers.

Mybizz CS answers all five with one connected platform at one monthly price, with no add-on fees.

The client is a business in one of Mybizz's markets (section 1). The United Kingdom is the home market and the focus of direct, personal client relationships; clients in the other markets are reached through content and digital marketing.

## 8. Brand Story

**Source:** `mb-3-cs-concept.md` §3, §11.2

Mybizz exists because its founder needed it and it did not exist.

After years of trying to build an online business presence, the founder met the same fragmented, expensive and technically demanding landscape that every small business meets: separate tools, repeated subscriptions, technical complexity, incomplete systems and no single place where the business could run properly.

The founder could not find one platform that solved the whole problem, so built it. Not part of the problem. All of it.

That story, "I built what I needed", is the brand's most durable asset. It is specific, honest and verifiable, and it connects directly with the client who has had the same experience. The brand's claim is not that Mybizz has features; it is that Mybizz was built by someone who lived the problem.

### The story in brief

> I built Mybizz because I needed it. I could not find one platform that gave a service business everything it needed without several subscriptions, technical setup, commissions or losing its own customers. So I built one.

## 12. Visual Identity

**Source:** `DESIGN.md` Design Direction Summary, Dual-Context Product Tone; `mb-3-cs-concept.md` §11.5

### Direction

Mybizz CS uses a clean, professional, business-focused design system built on the Anvil Material 3 theme with a small set of refinements, in two contexts (management and public), with dark mode following the device and Roboto typography.

The design communicates clarity, efficiency, trust, professional polish, calm confidence and modern reliability.

### Design principles

- Clarity over decoration.
- Consistency over experimentation.
- Reusable components over page-specific styling.
- Token-driven theming over hardcoded values.
- Layout variation through composition, not through parallel style systems.
- Public and management screens feel related, but not identical in tone: the management context is efficient and easy to scan; the public context is warm and inviting.

### Visual character

The interface is clean, structured, professional, trustworthy, efficient, calm, modern and polished.

### What the design avoids

- Bright gradients
- Flashy hero treatments
- Decorative visual noise
- Childish or playful interface language
- Heavy animation
- Glassmorphism and neumorphism
- Complex visuals that serve no business task
- Designing in dark mode first: light mode is the design baseline

## 13. Colour Strategy

**Source:** `DESIGN.md` Palette System; `mb-3-cs-concept.md` §11.5; `m3-custom-theme` (eight palettes, each light and dark)

### Mybizz's corporate colour

**Professional Blue** is Mybizz's own brand colour. It is used for mybizz.live, the Mybizz management app and everything Mybizz-Live Ltd publishes, and it is the default palette of every client instance.

Professional Blue carries the core promise of the brand: a dependable, trustworthy, professional business operating system. It suits consultants, clinics, therapists and professional-service businesses that need to project credibility.

### Why Professional Blue

- It communicates trust and reliability.
- It supports a serious, business-focused product.
- It works across consulting, wellness, professional services and operational screens.
- It does not make the product feel luxury-only or niche.
- It is a sound foundation for clear, high-contrast interfaces.

### The client's palette

Each client chooses its own brand palette at onboarding and can change it at any time. There are eight palettes, each a complete Material 3 colour scheme in light and dark: Default, Professional Blue, Warm Coral, Calm Teal, Sophisticated Purple, Sky Blue, Forest Green and Desert Rose. The chosen palette applies to every screen of the client's instance: public website, admin area and customer portal.

| Palette | Best fit | Intended effect |
|---|---|---|
| Professional Blue (default) | Consultants, clinics, therapists, professional-service businesses | Trust, professionalism, stability |
| Sophisticated Purple | Executive coaches, premium consultants, high-end services | Premium, refined, exclusive |
| Warm Coral | Salons, spas, beauty and lifestyle businesses | Warmth, creativity, approachability |
| Calm Teal | Fitness, wellness, physiotherapy, health services | Health, balance, growth |
| Sky Blue | IT support, online tutors, digital and remote services | Fresh, open, modern |
| Forest Green | Gardeners, trades, outdoor and sustainability services, financial advisers | Growth, nature, dependability |
| Desert Rose | Aestheticians, make-up artists, photographers, event planners | Elegant, warm, personal |
| Default | Businesses that want the plain Material 3 look | Neutral, standard |

Sophisticated Purple is the strongest alternative for premium positioning: executive coaching, premium consulting, high-end professional services and luxury wellness or lifestyle brands that want a more exclusive, refined identity.

### Colour principles

- Colour supports hierarchy; it does not decorate.
- The primary colour is used sparingly, for important actions and emphasis.
- Neutral surfaces dominate the interface.
- Colours come only from the palettes; there is no free colour picker.
- Status is never shown by colour alone: every status badge carries its status word.

## 16. Interaction Principles

**Source:** `DESIGN.md` Input and State Rules, Component and Role Map, Motion and Interaction Guidance

### Buttons

| Button | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Primary (filled) | The main action on a screen |
| Secondary (outlined) | Supporting or alternative actions |
| Tertiary (text) | Low-emphasis actions |
| Icon button | Compact utility actions |
| Floating action button | Creating a new primary item |

### State clarity

Every interactive element makes its state obvious:

- The default state is clear and unambiguous.
- The focus state is always visible.
- The error state is noticeable without being alarming.
- The disabled state shows plainly that an action is unavailable.
- The read-only state is never confused with the disabled state.

### Feedback

The product gives clear feedback when the user saves changes, submits a form, completes a booking, makes a payment, meets an error, waits for a system process or reaches an empty state. Motion is subtle and used only where it helps.

## 20. Customer Journeys

A visitor to a client's website:

| Option | Cost |

- `demo.mybizz.live`: a working business with sample data
- `try.mybizz.live`: an empty Mybizz CS for trying setup

Both reset nightly, and their payments run in test mode.

### Costs outside the price

The client registers and owns its domain name, pays its payment providers' own fees, and holds a free Cloudflare account in its own name for its backups; storage above Cloudflare's free allowance is billed by Cloudflare to the client. Hosting, email, AI text and AI voice are paid by Mybizz.

## 23. Trust, Security and Ownership

**Source:** `business-requirements-document.md`; `product-requirements-document.md` FR-01, FR-30, Email delivery; [[[default:specifications-global/spec-regulatory-compliance-baseline|Regulatory Compliance Baseline]]](C:/dev/project-library-global/specifications-global/spec-regulatory-compliance-baseline.md)

Trust is a central theme of the brand. The client stays in control of its business, customers, data, credentials and online presence.

### Ownership promises

- Each client has its own isolated application instance and database.
- No client's data is shared with any other client.
- Clients export their data as CSV.
- Backups and history are kept in the client's own Cloudflare storage.
- A client leaves with its data, emails and backups.
- Mybizz runs no marketplace and no shared client directory.

### Security promises

- Every account holder signs in with a second factor; roles are enforced on the server.
- Credentials are encrypted in the Vault, and viewing one needs a fresh TOTP code.
- Sign-in is rate-limited, with account lockout.
- Significant actions are recorded in an audit trail.
- Customer records are open only to authorised users.
- The platform never stores card data; payments are taken by Stripe and PayPal.

### Privacy and data protection

- Mybizz-Live Ltd is subject to the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and registers with the Information Commissioner's Office before launch.

- All work on client data is done from the United Kingdom or from Mybizz's markets.
- A personal data breach is reported to the affected client without undue delay, so that the client can meet its own 72-hour duty.

## 28. Visual Do's and Don'ts

**Source:** `DESIGN.md` Do Emulate, Do Not Emulate

### Do

- Use clean, structured layouts.
- Keep a consistent visual hierarchy.
- Use white space deliberately.
- Use cards to organise related content.
- Keep navigation predictable.
- Use subtle motion only where it helps.
- Make every call to action clear.
- Support light and dark modes.
- Keep text readable in every palette and every language.

### Don't

- Use bright gradients or flashy hero treatments.
- Use childish or playful language.
- Use heavy animation, glassmorphism or neumorphism.
- Add decorative visual noise.
- Build complex visuals that serve no business task.
- Use colour as the only carrier of meaning.
- Let styling differ unpredictably between templates.
