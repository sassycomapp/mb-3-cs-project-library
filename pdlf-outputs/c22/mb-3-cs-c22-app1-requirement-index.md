# Step 22 — Requirement Index (run app1, route Application)

Every form the tickets name, by module path, in the tickets' build order, with its ticket, its requirement's path and its access-matrix row (present, or added at this step). Sub-forms nest under their host form's module path — the rule David set at Step 22; ticket 34's homepage-template paths and `dashboard.MetricCard`'s path were corrected at source to match it. Requirement paths follow nomenclature section 1.7: `ui/<package path>/<FormName>/<FormName>-requirement.md`, mirroring the module path.

Decisions recorded at Step 22 (David's):
- The three campaign forms (`crm.EmailCampaignListForm`, `crm.EmailCampaignEditorForm`, `crm.EmailBroadcastForm`) are added to ticket 29 (F-18's build step); ticket 29 and the c19 ticket list were corrected at source.
- Every local template, tab, view and step form nests under its host form's module path; ticket 34's homepage-template paths were restated at source under `public_pages.HomePage.`.
- `dashboard.MetricCard` and `dashboard.ActivityFeed` move to `dashboard.DashboardForm.MetricCard` / `dashboard.DashboardForm.ActivityFeed` when dashboard.DashboardForm is built (they exist today as siblings at `dashboard.MetricCard` / `dashboard.ActivityFeed`); the index records the move, the build makes it.
- The flow-51 card's step-1 label was corrected at source (Step 21's deferred item): "1 Password · FIDO2 enrol" is now "1 Welcome · Orientation" — onboarding step 1 is Welcome and orientation; password setting and FIDO2 enrolment happen at first sign-in, before the wizard.
