# legal-local

The client's Owner is solely responsible for the content of the client's Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions, and the application's clause builder helps the Owner write them: [[[default:adr-global/adr-legal-policy-responsibility-acknowledgement-and-clause-builder-architecture]]](C:/dev/project-library-global/adr-global/adr-legal-policy-responsibility-acknowledgement-and-clause-builder-architecture.md). This folder holds:

| Document | What it is |
|---|---|
| `clause-library-privacy-policy.md` | The default clauses the clause builder offers for the Privacy Policy |
| `clause-library-terms-and-conditions.md` | The default clauses for the Terms and Conditions |
| `clause-library-cookie-policy.md` | The default clauses for the Cookie Policy |
| `client-service-agreement.md` | The agreement between Mybizz and the client |
| `data-processing-agreement.md` | How Mybizz processes the client's customer data on the client's behalf, as the UK GDPR requires |
| `compliance-record.md` | The application's UK GDPR, client privacy law and PCI DSS position and its dated obligations |

The released copies are kept with each release in `docs-local\releases\v{n}`. The requirements are in [[[default:specifications-global/spec-regulatory-compliance-baseline]]](C:/dev/project-library-global/specifications-global/spec-regulatory-compliance-baseline.md).
