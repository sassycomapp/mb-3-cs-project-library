# Mybizz Consulting Services — Terms of Service in Respect of Paddle

These terms form part of the client service agreement. They set out how a client buys and pays for its Mybizz subscription through Paddle.

## Paddle as Reseller and Merchant of Record

Mybizz's order process is conducted by its online reseller, Paddle.com Market Ltd and its affiliated companies ("Paddle"). Paddle is the Merchant of Record for every order: when the client buys a Mybizz subscription, the client buys it from Paddle.

Paddle is responsible for:

- the checkout, payment processing and the monthly renewal of the subscription;
- charging and paying any VAT or sales tax due in the client's country;
- issuing a valid tax invoice or receipt for every payment;
- order-related questions: payment, invoices, refunds and changes of card.

Mybizz is responsible for the Mybizz service itself, its onboarding and all product support.

## Invoices and Receipts

- Paddle emails the client a tax invoice or receipt for every payment, including every monthly renewal.
- The invoice names Paddle.com Market Ltd as the seller, with Paddle's tax registration numbers, and carries the Mybizz name and logo.
- A business client can enter its business name and VAT or tax number at checkout. Where the number is valid and the reverse charge applies, Paddle charges no VAT. A client that did not enter its number can add it from the link in the receipt email, and Paddle reissues the invoice.
- The client finds every past invoice, changes its card and cancels through Paddle's customer portal, reached from every Paddle email.
- The client's card statement shows Paddle's name together with Mybizz's.

## Refunds

- No refund is given for part of a month, as the client service agreement sets out.
- Where Mybizz agrees to a refund, Paddle pays it to the original payment method on Mybizz's instruction.

## Price Changes

- The subscription renews each month at the price the client agreed.
- Mybizz gives the client at least 30 days' notice by email before any change to the price. The new price applies from the first renewal after the notice period ends. A client that does not accept the change may cancel before it takes effect, under the client service agreement.
