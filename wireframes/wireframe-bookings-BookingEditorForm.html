Round-Trip Budget

3 server calls maximum (ADR-035):
RT1: get_booking_context(service_id) — returns service details, available providers, date ranges, intake form template
RT2: get_available_slots(provider_id, date_range) — returns available time slots
RT3: confirm_booking(booking_data) — processes payment and creates booking

Steps 1-2 use RT1 data. Step 3 triggers RT2. Steps 4-5 are client-side from RT1 cache. Step 6 triggers RT3. Step 7 is client-side.
