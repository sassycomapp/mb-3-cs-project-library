Architecture Note

Onboarding is a separate form — not embedded in Settings, but links to Settings for specific tasks.
Settings is a single tabbed form — uses M3 buttons for tab navigation, RBAC-governed.
Bidirectional navigation — Onboarding has buttons to jump to Settings tabs; Settings has a "Return to Onboarding" button.
Onboarding is resumable — Owner may leave and return without losing progress. [ADR-023]
Payment gateway is NOT in onboarding — it is a Settings concern. [ADR-019]
Legal acknowledgement belongs to onboarding — not to mutable Settings configuration. [ADR-018, ADR-025]

Settings Navigation (Bidirectional)

Onboarding → Settings
The onboarding form has three buttons that link to specific Settings tabs:

Step 2 — Business Profile: "Configure in Settings →" opens SettingsForm with Business Profile tab active.
Step 3 — Palette Selection: "Choose Palette in Settings →" opens SettingsForm with Palette tab active.
Header — "Go to Settings": Opens SettingsForm (default tab).

When navigating from onboarding to Settings, the Settings form's "Return to Onboarding" button is visible.
Settings → Onboarding
The Settings form includes a "Return to Onboarding" button in the header:

Visibility: Visible only when business_profile.onboarding_status == "in_progress"
Action: Returns to OnboardingForm with progress preserved
After completion: Button is hidden (onboarding_status == "completed")

See wireframe-settings-SettingsForm.html for the Settings side of this navigation.

Behaviour Notes

Onboarding Behaviour
1. Resumability: Onboarding is resumable. The Owner may leave at any time (navigating to Settings or closing the browser) and return without losing progress. The current step is stored in business_profile.onboarding_step. [ADR-023]

2. Revisitable: After completion, the Owner may return to onboarding to review or change credentials. The form loads in review mode with all steps accessible. [ADR-025]

3. Required Elements: System Currency (step 4) and Legal Acknowledgement (step 5) are required. The "Continue" button on these steps is disabled until the required action is completed.

4. Optional Links: Business Profile (step 2) and Palette Selection (step 3) link to Settings tabs. These steps can be skipped during onboarding and completed later in Settings.

5. Payment Gateway: NOT part of onboarding. Payment gateway is configured in Settings → Payment Gateway tab. Onboarding completion does not require gateway setup. [ADR-019]

6. System Currency Immutability: Once confirmed AND the first transaction is recorded, system_currency becomes immutable. Before the first transaction, the Owner may change the selection. [ADR-016]

7. Legal Acknowledgement Finality: Legal and data governance acknowledgements are recorded once and cannot be reversed. The confirmation is logged in the Mybizz_management amendment log. [ADR-018, ADR-025]

8. Progress Indicator: A visual progress bar and step counter are always visible. Completed steps show green, current step shows dark, future steps show grey.

9. Onboarding Completion: Onboarding is complete when System Currency has been confirmed and Legal Acknowledgement has been confirmed. After completion:
  - business_profile.onboarding_status = "completed"
  - business_profile.onboarding_completed = True
  - business_profile.onboarding_completed_at = datetime.now()
  - The Owner is directed to the Dashboard
  - The "Return to Onboarding" button in Settings is hidden
