ContactPage

Public-facing contact form — submits to contact_submissions table

Section 1 — Introduction

To the developer: This wireframe indicates which components to place on the ContactPage form. ContactPage is a public-facing form that submits visitor enquiries to the contact_submissions table. Uses FooterComponent.

Conventions: Solid border = visible on load. Dashed border = hidden on load. Each component shows: component_name (Type) "fixed text"
