5 Tips for Better Tax Planning
hdg_title (Heading) "5 Tips for Better Tax Planning"
By Sarah Johnson · May 10, 2026 · 5 min read
txt_meta (Text)
[Featured Image]
img_featured (Image)

Tax planning is one of the most important aspects of running a successful business. Whether you're a sole proprietor or running a growing company, understanding how to optimize your tax position can save you thousands of rands each year.

txt_content (RichText)

1. Start Early

The best tax planning happens well before the tax deadline. Start reviewing your financial position at least 3 months before year-end.

2. Keep Accurate Records

Maintain detailed records of all business expenses. This includes receipts, invoices, and bank statements.

3. Understand Deductions

Know which expenses are deductible and which are not. Common deductions include:

  • Office supplies and equipment
  • Business travel expenses
  • Professional development
  • Insurance premiums

4. Plan for Retirement Contributions

Retirement contributions are tax-deductible. Consider maximizing your contributions to reduce taxable income.

5. Consult a Professional

Tax laws change frequently. Working with a qualified tax professional ensures you're taking advantage of all available deductions and credits.
