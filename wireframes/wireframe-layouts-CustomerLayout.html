Usage

CustomerLayout is the shell for the customer portal (V2).
Customers book via the public site and the business manages all bookings from the admin panel in V1.
The customer portal is deferred — this wireframe documents the intended structure for future implementation.
Navigation items: My Dashboard, My Bookings, My Invoices, My Reviews, Support Tickets, Account, Sign Out.
