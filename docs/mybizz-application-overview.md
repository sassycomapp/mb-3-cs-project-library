# Mybizz — Application Overview

**mybizz.live**
Version 1.0 | 2026

## 1. Executive Summary

Mybizz is a complete online business platform built specifically for consulting and services businesses. It replaces the fragmented collection of separate tools — website builders, booking systems, payment processors, CRMs, email platforms, and invoicing software — with a single, unified platform at a single price.

Every Mybizz subscriber receives their own fully operational business platform: a professional website, a booking and scheduling system, integrated payment processing, a customer database, email marketing, invoicing, a blog, and business analytics. Everything works together from day one. Nothing needs to be connected, configured separately, or paid for as an add-on.

---

## 2. The Problem

Running a professional service business online today means stitching together five or six separate tools — each with its own subscription, its own learning curve, and its own way of storing your data. A typical setup might look like this:

- **A website builder** — one subscription, one login, one set of design decisions
- **A booking system** — another subscription, another integration to figure out
- **A payment gateway** — another signup, another configuration process
- **A CRM or contact spreadsheet** — yet another tool, or worse, a manual process
- **An email marketing platform** — another account to manage, another list to keep in sync
- **An invoicing tool** — one more service, one more monthly fee

Each tool costs money on its own. None of them share data cleanly. Client information ends up entered three times in three different places. A booking in one system does not automatically create an invoice in another. An enquiry on the website does not automatically appear in the CRM. And the business owner ends the day with six browser tabs open, manually moving information between tools that should have been designed to work together.

Most small service businesses never get all of it working. They settle for a partial setup — perhaps a booking link and a social media page — and manage everything else by hand. The cost is not just financial. It is time, missed opportunities, unprofessional client experiences, and no reliable view of how the business is actually performing.

---

## 3. The Solution

Mybizz brings everything into one place. A business owner signs up once, sets up their business once, and then runs everything from a single platform.

A typical day on Mybizz looks like this: a client finds the business through the website, browses the services, books an appointment, and pays — all in one seamless flow. The booking appears on the business's calendar automatically. A confirmation email goes out immediately. A reminder is sent 24 hours before and again 1 hour before the appointment. The client's details are captured in the customer database. An invoice is generated. And the business owner can see it all — revenue, bookings, new customers, upcoming appointments — on a single dashboard the moment they log in.

There is nothing to connect. Nothing to sync. Nothing to troubleshoot across multiple platforms. The website, the booking system, the payment processing, the customer records, the email communications, the invoices, and the analytics all share the same data because they are all part of the same platform.

One setup. One platform. One price. Everything included.

## 4. Core Capabilities

### 4.1 Onboarding and Setup

Mybizz onboarding is designed to get a business live as quickly as possible, without requiring technical knowledge.

- **Guided first-run experience** — a step-by-step walkthrough that covers every essential setup task in the correct order, with a visible progress indicator throughout
- **Business profile setup** — business name, description, contact details, address, logo, social media links, and accreditations
- **Brand palette selection** — four professionally designed colour palettes to choose from, each suited to different business types (consultants, salons, wellness studios, premium services), with a live preview of how the choice affects the entire platform
- **Currency selection** — choose the business's primary currency at setup; all pricing, invoicing, and reporting use this currency consistently
- **Legal compliance acknowledgement** — guided acknowledgement of data governance responsibilities, recorded for audit purposes
- **Payment gateway connection** — connect either Stripe (for global payments) or Paystack (for South African and African clients) with simple key entry
- **Email configuration** — connect the business's own email sending account for transactional and marketing emails
- **30-minute guided setup call** — included with every subscription; the Mybizz founder personally walks each new client through their initial configuration
- **Premium done-for-you setup** — for business owners who prefer it, Mybizz will configure the entire platform on their behalf

### 4.2 Dashboard and Business Analytics

The dashboard is the first thing a business owner sees after logging in. It provides an immediate, at-a-glance view of business performance.

- **Revenue overview** — total revenue for the current period with comparison to the previous period
- **Booking count** — number of bookings in the current period with trend comparison
- **Customer totals** — total active customers and new customers in the current period
- **Activity feed** — recent activity across the platform: new bookings, payments received, new contacts, campaign activity
- **Metric cards** — individual KPI cards for revenue, bookings, and customer counts, each showing the current value, percentage change, and trend direction
- **Period comparison** — all metrics support comparison against the previous period (week, month, or custom range)
- **Visual charts** — simple, clear revenue and booking trend charts for quick pattern recognition

### 4.3 Bookings and Scheduling

The booking system is the operational heart of any service business. Mybizz handles the complete booking lifecycle.

- **Full booking flow** — customers select a service, choose a provider, pick a date and time, select a meeting type, complete any required intake forms, and pay — all in one continuous flow
- **Calendar views** — month, week, and day calendar views for managing the business schedule at different levels of detail
- **Availability management** — set business hours by day of the week, configure provider-specific schedules, and block out dates for holidays or closures
- **Availability exceptions** — override regular hours for specific dates (e.g., a provider taking a day off, extended hours for a special event)
- **Meeting types** — every service can offer one or more meeting formats: in-person, video call, or phone call; video call links are generated automatically
- **Payment at booking** — collect full payment or a deposit at the time of booking, configurable per service
- **Automatic reminders** — email reminders sent 24 hours and 1 hour before each appointment, including video call links where applicable
- **Recurring appointments** — support for weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly recurring bookings
- **Booking viewer** — detailed view of any booking showing client information, service details, payment status, and available actions
- **Booking status management** — bookings move through clear statuses (pending, confirmed, cancelled) with appropriate actions available at each stage
- **Reschedule support** — confirmed bookings can be rescheduled without cancellation
- **Multi-provider support** — each provider (staff member) has their own calendar, their own services, and their own availability; revenue is tracked per provider
- **Special requests** — customers can add notes or special requests when booking

### 4.4 Service Catalogue Management

Mybizz supports two pricing models to cover the full range of service businesses.

- **Time-based pricing** — services priced by duration (e.g., a 60-minute massage at a set rate)
- **Deliverable pricing** — services priced as a fixed deliverable (e.g., a logo design at a fixed price)
- **Service editor** — full creation and editing of services with name, description, category, pricing model, price, duration, images, video URL, meeting type, and active/inactive status
- **Service categories** — organize services into categories for easy browsing on the public website
- **Provider assignment** — assign one or more providers to each service; customers can choose their preferred provider or be assigned to any available one
- **Per-service intake forms** — optionally require customers to complete a custom intake form when booking a specific service
- **Video embeds** — add video content to individual service pages (e.g., a walkthrough of what to expect during a treatment)
- **Service viewer** — detailed read-only view of any service showing all configuration, assigned providers, and related bookings

### 4.5 Customer Relationship Management

The CRM is a unified system for managing every person who interacts with the business — leads, active customers, and past customers.

- **Unified contact database** — a single, centralised record for every person: leads, active customers, and inactive customers, all in one place with a clear status indicator
- **Contact lifecycle tracking** — every contact has a status (Lead, Customer, or Inactive) that reflects their current relationship with the business
- **Full activity timeline** — see every interaction a contact has had with the business: bookings made, emails received, notes added, forms submitted — all in chronological order
- **Contact detail fields** — name, email, phone, source, tags, internal notes, preferences, lifetime value, total bookings, average transaction value, and last contact date
- **Automatic contact creation** — contacts are created automatically when someone makes a booking or submits an enquiry form; no manual data entry required
- **Tags and segmentation** — tag contacts manually or let the system categorize them automatically based on their behaviour
- **Pre-built segments** — ready-to-use customer segments: New Leads, Active Customers, Inactive Customers, and High Value customers
- **Custom segment builder** — create custom segments using filters for tags, status, total spend, and booking history
- **CSV import and export** — import existing customer lists from spreadsheets and export the full database at any time
- **Contact list** — searchable, filterable list of all contacts with quick access to their profile, activity history, and actions
- **Contact editor and viewer** — full create, edit, and read-only views for every contact record

### 4.6 Email Marketing and Campaigns

Mybizz includes a complete email marketing system — not a basic notification tool, but a real marketing engine with sequences, broadcasts, and tracking.

- **Transactional email** — automatic emails for booking confirmations, appointment reminders, invoice delivery, and password resets; branded with the business's name and email address
- **Campaign sequences** — automated email sequences triggered by events: new client welcome series, appointment follow-up, re-engagement for inactive customers, and referral requests
- **Campaign editor** — create and configure email campaigns with configurable delays between emails and event-based enrollment triggers
- **Email broadcasts** — send one-time email broadcasts to selected segments or the full contact list
- **Segment-based targeting** — send campaigns to specific customer segments (e.g., a re-engagement campaign only to customers who have not booked in 90 days)
- **Open and click tracking** — track which emails are opened and which links are clicked, with results visible in the campaign dashboard
- **Campaign metrics** — view campaign-level performance: emails sent, opens, clicks, conversions, and revenue generated
- **Email templates** — manage reusable email templates for common communications
- **Sender configuration** — configure the business's sending name and email address; test the connection to verify setup

### 4.7 Invoicing and Payments

The invoicing and payments system handles the full financial transaction lifecycle, from booking to payment to receipt.

- **Automatic invoice generation** — when a booking is completed, an invoice is generated automatically, including all service details, line items, and totals
- **Manual invoice creation** — create invoices manually for ad-hoc services, packages, or custom work
- **Invoice editor** — full invoice creation with customer selection, date fields, line item entry (description, quantity, unit price), notes, and currency
- **Invoice viewer** — detailed invoice view showing invoice number, status badge, bill-to information, line items table, subtotal, tax, and total
- **Invoice lifecycle management** — invoices move through clear statuses: draft, sent, paid, overdue, and cancelled, with appropriate actions available at each stage
- **PDF generation** — invoices are available as downloadable PDF documents
- **Email delivery** — send invoices directly to customers via email with a single action
- **Payment recording** — record payments against invoices, automatically updating the invoice status
- **Payment gateway integration** — payments processed through Stripe (global) or Paystack (South Africa and Africa), integrated directly into the booking flow
- **Gateway-agnostic design** — both payment gateways use the same underlying service, so the experience is identical regardless of which gateway the business uses
- **Multi-currency support** — the business sets its primary currency at onboarding; optional display currency for regional pricing
- **Invoice list** — searchable, filterable list of all invoices with status filtering and quick actions
- **Invoice numbering** — automatic sequential invoice numbering (e.g., INV-2026-001) for professional documentation

### 4.8 Content and Blog

The blog system provides a full content publishing platform for businesses that want to build authority, share expertise, or drive organic traffic.

- **Rich text editor** — write and format blog posts with a full-featured editor supporting headings, lists, images, links, and embedded video
- **Blog post templates** — three layout options for each post: classic centred (best for long-form articles), two-column with sidebar (best for engagement), and full-width immersive (best for visual storytelling)
- **SEO control per post** — set a custom SEO title, meta description, URL slug, and keywords for every post
- **Featured images and video** — add a featured image and embed video content (YouTube) in individual posts
- **Pullquotes** — highlight key quotes with featured pullquote styling in full-width posts
- **Post statuses** — manage posts as Draft, Published, or Archived; only published posts appear on the public site
- **Blog categories** — organize posts into categories with management tools for creating, editing, and ordering categories
- **View count tracking** — see how many times each post has been viewed
- **Blog list views** — the public blog listing supports two view modes: a tiled grid view for visual browsing and a list with thumbnails for editorial scanning
- **Comment moderation** — manage blog comments with approval, spam, and deletion controls; comments can be enabled or disabled globally
- **Public blog pages** — automatically generated blog listing and individual post pages, fully integrated with the public website

### 4.9 Public Website and Landing Pages

Every Mybizz subscriber gets a complete, professional, mobile-responsive public website — managed entirely from the admin panel.

- **Homepage** — choose from four professionally designed homepage layouts: Classic (balanced, general-purpose), Services (services-first display), Booking (conversion-focused with prominent call-to-action), and Minimalist (clean, minimal, whitespace-driven)
- **Homepage sections** — every homepage draws from a shared set of sections: hero area with parallax effect, services showcase carousel, about summary, video introduction, customer testimonials, trust and credibility strip, call-to-action band, and footer
- **About page** — tell the business's story with a dedicated, professionally laid out about page
- **Contact page** — a public contact form that captures enquiries directly into the CRM, with Google Maps integration for showing the business location
- **Services page** — an automatically generated listing of all active services with detail pages, images, and booking links
- **Privacy Policy page** — a professionally structured privacy policy page, managed through a clause-based editor with pre-seeded templates
- **Terms and Conditions page** — a professionally structured terms page, managed the same way as the privacy policy
- **Landing pages** — create dedicated landing pages for specific marketing campaigns using three templates: Lead Capture (for collecting contact information), Event Registration (for promoting and registering attendees), and Video Sales Letter (for video-driven conversion)
- **Landing page analytics** — track views and conversions for each landing page
- **Footer** — a consistent, professional footer across all public pages showing business credentials, social links, contact information, policy links, and optional Google Maps embed
- **Mobile responsive** — every public page is fully responsive and looks professional on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **SEO optimised** — all public pages include proper meta tags, semantic structure, and clean URLs for search engine visibility
- **Custom domain support** — businesses can connect their own domain name, or use a Mybizz subdomain at no additional cost

### 4.10 Settings, Customization, and Branding

Settings is a single, tabbed control panel for all business configuration, organized by concern with role-based access control.

- **Business Profile** — manage business name, email, phone, address, timezone, entity type, social media links, accreditations, and logo
- **Brand palette** — switch between four professionally designed colour palettes at any time; the change applies instantly across the entire platform
- **Homepage template** — switch between homepage layout templates from settings
- **Blog settings** — configure the default blog list view (tiled or list), enable or disable comments, and manage blog-related preferences
- **Video content** — add or change video URLs for the homepage and services page
- **System currency** — view the primary currency setting (immutable after the first transaction to ensure financial consistency)
- **Display currency** — optionally configure a different display currency for specific services or regions
- **Payment gateway** — configure and manage the active payment gateway (Stripe or Paystack); test mode toggle for sandbox testing
- **Email configuration** — set up and verify the business's email sending identity; test the connection
- **User management** — invite team members, assign roles (Manager, Admin, Staff, Customer), manage access, activate and deactivate accounts
- **Feature toggles** — enable or disable platform features like marketing tools and the blog, so the interface only shows what the business actually uses
- **Theme customization** — fine-tune the public website's visual presentation with colour and font options
- **Legal policy editor** — manage Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions using a structured, clause-based editor with pre-seeded templates that can be customized, reordered, and published
- **Return to onboarding** — if initial setup was not completed, a direct link back to the onboarding flow is always available

### 4.11 Tasks and Productivity

Mybizz includes a task management system to help business owners stay on top of follow-ups and operational actions.

- **Manual task creation** — create tasks linked to specific contacts with a title, due date, and notes
- **Auto-generated tasks** — the system automatically creates follow-up tasks based on business activity (e.g., following up with a new lead, requesting a review after an appointment)
- **Task list** — a centralized task list with completion tracking, filtering, and due date visibility
- **Task completion** — mark tasks as completed with automatic timestamping for audit trail

### 4.12 Lead Capture and Marketing Automation

Beyond email campaigns, Mybizz includes tools specifically designed to capture and convert leads.

- **Lead capture forms** — create dedicated lead capture forms for landing pages with customizable fields and offer text
- **Automatic contact creation** — when a lead submits a form, both the lead record and the contact record are created simultaneously; the lead is immediately linked to the contact with no manual conversion step
- **Lead tracking** — track leads from their source (landing page, enquiry form, manual entry) through to conversion as a paying customer
- **Capture analytics** — see how many leads each capture form has generated and how many have converted
- **Campaign enrollment** — automatically enroll new leads into welcome sequences or nurture campaigns

### 4.13 Security and Data Protection

Mybizz is built with security as a foundational requirement, not an afterthought. Every aspect of data protection is designed to give business owners confidence that their business data — and their customers' data — is safe.

- **Bank-level credential protection** — all sensitive credentials (payment gateway keys, email passwords, API tokens) are encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption. They are never visible to anyone — not even to Mybizz support — after they are saved
- **Two-factor authentication for sensitive operations** — accessing stored credentials requires a time-based verification code from an authenticator app, entered every single time with no "remember me" shortcut. An email notification is sent to the business owner on every access
- **Five-tier access control** — the platform supports five distinct access levels: Owner (full control), Manager (operational management), Admin (booking and customer management), Staff (own schedule only), and Customer (own bookings and invoices). Every action on the platform is governed by the user's assigned role
- **Server-enforced permissions** — access control is enforced on the server side, not just in the visible interface. A user cannot access data or perform actions outside their role, regardless of how they interact with the platform
- **Rate limiting** — the platform limits the number of requests any user or IP address can make, protecting against automated attacks and abuse
- **Full audit trail** — every significant action on the platform is logged with the user, timestamp, and description for accountability and compliance
- **Data isolation** — every business's data is completely isolated from every other business. No business can see another business's clients, bookings, revenue, or any other information. This isolation is built into the platform's fundamental structure, not achieved through filters or configuration
- **Compliance alignment** — the platform is designed to support POPIA (South Africa), GDPR (international), and PCI DSS (payment card industry) compliance requirements
- **Data ownership** — every business owns its data completely. Client lists, booking history, contact details, and transaction records belong exclusively to the subscribing business. Data can be exported at any time. If a business cancels, their data does not remain accessible to anyone else

## 7. Why Mybizz CS

### The Ownership Argument

On marketplace platforms like Booksy, Vagaro, or Mindbody, the business is listed alongside its competitors. The platform's algorithm decides visibility. New client acquisition often comes with commissions of up to 20 percent per transaction. And if the business leaves the platform, the client relationship stays behind.

On Mybizz, the business has its own standalone platform. No competitors appear on the business's pages. No algorithm affects visibility. No commission is taken on bookings. The client database belongs exclusively to the business — structurally isolated, privately hosted, and accessible only by the business owner. Clients are the business's clients on day one and remain the business's clients permanently.

### The Total Cost Argument

Marketplace platforms advertise low base prices. The real cost tells a different story. Per-staff fees, processing commissions, marketplace cuts, add-ons for features like SMS or forms, and the cost of a separate website hosted elsewhere — a typical service business on a marketplace platform pays significantly more than the headline price suggests, and still does not own its data or have a complete integrated platform.

Mybizz is all-inclusive. Website, hosting, booking system, payment processing, CRM, email marketing, invoicing, blog, and analytics — all included in a single subscription. Nothing is extra. Nothing is an add-on. The price is the price.

### The Simplicity Argument

Most service businesses are running their online operations across five or six separate tools. Each one costs money. None of them talk to each other properly. Mybizz replaces all of them.

Set up the business once. Website, booking, payments, CRM, email, invoicing — all connected, all working, all in one place. Then run the business.

### The Africa Advantage

Stripe does not operate in South Africa. Booksy and Vagaro were not built for the African market. Mybizz integrates Paystack natively — clients pay in Rands, the money lands in the business's bank account, no workarounds. This positions Mybizz as the only complete business platform built specifically for African service businesses.

### The Founder Story

Mybizz was not designed by a product team optimizing metrics. It was built by a founder who spent years trying to establish an online service business and encountered exactly the fragmentation, cost, and complexity described above. After speaking with other small business owners, the pattern was consistent: the challenge was not ambition or effort — it was the sheer fragmentation of the tools required to operate a modern service business online.

Mybizz is the product the founder needed and could not find. That origin is the product's most credible differentiator: it was designed by someone who lived the problem, not someone who observed it from the outside.
