# Scope Card — mb-3-cs-b16-app1-domain-map (re-scoped)

**intent:** Show Mybizz CS's domain — the glossary's entities and their relationships — so a reader trusts that every entity has one owner and one place in the data design, and that the trade/common marking holds. The load-bearing claim: **the contact is the spine every trade record links to — the one direction the rule allows — and Mybizz's own billing (plan summary, referrals) is written only from the App 5 and Paddle side, never becoming a money record of the client's business.** Audience: David at the gate, then Step 17, which builds the tables from this vocabulary.
**type:** architecture — entities and their ownership and relationships; the default for "everything that exists and connects"; not a flow or a state picture.
**quality_profile:** standard
**node inventory (11):** client-business *(common* — the subscribing business, owner of every record below it*) · contact* *(common* — the customer as one person; the spine every trade record links to*) · provider* *(common* — the user who delivers services*) · service* *(trade* — the catalogue offering, bookable or unit-priced*) · booking* *(trade* — of a service or package; sessions drawn down*) · order* *(trade* — delivered work, with its brief and delivery*) · quote* *(trade* — the offer whose acceptance creates the invoice*) · invoice* *(trade* — the money claim, with its lines and tax*) · payment* *(trade* — money taken or refunded, by gateway or by hand*) · crm-outreach* *(common* — one node for the cluster: leads, enquiries, segments, campaigns, tasks, email; deals stay trade, deferred to a card*) · mybizz-billing* *(common* — plan, subscription, referral, plan summary, written from the App 5 and Paddle side*)
**main path:** contact → booking → invoice → payment — the trade's money path. Side branches: service → booking (top centre); provider → booking (bottom centre); order, quote → invoice; crm-outreach → contact; mybizz-billing ↔ client-business.
**deferred to cards:** Customer package — the card states that a booking draws down one session of a customer package; Resource; Group session and Place; Recurring series; Availability rules and exceptions, Slot; Meeting type; Intake; Time entry; Deposit, Tax; Expense and Expense category; Company and Deal (deal trade); Contact event and Timeline; Automation rule and run; Enrolment; Email address and Desk; Suppression; Review and Testimonial; the public-website cluster (Page, Blog post, Landing page, Media file); the legal cluster (Legal document, Consent record); the platform cluster (Client instance, Release image, Vault, Notification). Each card names its glossary term; the full inventory lives in `docs-local\GLOSSARY.md` and `authoritative-schema.md`.
**corridor plan:** architecture is free-form — no stage/row grid. Layout: the trade spine left to right (contact → booking → invoice → payment); service above the spine, a straight vertical via the empty band into booking's top centre; **provider directly below booking and quote directly below invoice, each a straight vertical into the bottom centre; no edge crosses another** (David's visual-check ruling); crm-outreach below contact, centre-aligned; client-business as the owner band above all; mybizz-billing at the right edge, outside the client band, its one edge to client-business routed above the feeding row. Every node's sublabel begins with its mark, "common · " or "trade · ".
**layout budget source:** renderers/architecture/ — no README with a layout budget exists for this type (noted for the receipt); the budget is the renderer's own free-form grid (grid.mjs), read at authoring.
**probe declaration:** expected corridors — the three below-spine verticals (x 180, 540, 955) and the two above-spine verticals (x 335–540 route, x 955) run in empty bands; no edge crosses another. Abort conditions: node overlap, an edge through a node, an edge crossing an edge, or an ambiguous shared corridor surviving the repair order.
**decisions (verbatim):** David approved the card with two corrections on 2026-11-12 — "1. Mark contact, provider and crm-outreach common, as the plan's §14.9 marks them: contacts and users are common tables, F-17 is common, and leads, enquiries, segments, campaigns, tasks and email are common tables (deals stay trade, among the deferred cards). The load-bearing claim stands: the trade records link to the common contact, the one direction the rule allows. 2. Before the gate, bring the plan's §14.9 up to date for F-37: add F-37 to the common features, add plan_summary to the common tables, and change 'Common (50)' to 'Common (51)'." — re-scoped it the same day after the first authoring attempt returned corridor failures: "Neither A, B nor C as offered. Run mb-archify-scope's recovery as the skill sets out. The re-scope: move customer-package from the node inventory to the deferred-to-cards list (11 nodes); keep service→booking at booking's top centre and provider→booking at its bottom centre; booking's sublabel becomes 'of a service or package; sessions drawn down'; the Customer package card states that a booking draws down one session of a customer package." — and, on his visual review of the first delivery, recorded two layout corrections within the approved card: "1. Provider's and quote's edges cross the main path (provider's vertical crosses booking→invoice beside the 'billed as' label; quote's crosses invoice→payment beside 'settled by'). Move provider and quote into the row below the spine: provider directly below booking, quote directly below invoice, each edge a straight vertical into the bottom centre. No edge may cross another. 2. The common/trade marks do not appear on the map. Start every node's sublabel with its mark, 'common · ' or 'trade · ', as on the approved card (common: client-business, contact, provider, crm-outreach, mybizz-billing; trade: service, booking, order, quote, invoice, payment)."
**status:** approved
